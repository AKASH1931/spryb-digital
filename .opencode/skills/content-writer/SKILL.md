# Marketing Content Writer Skill

Top-notch agency copywriter voice for Spryb Digital. English only. Apply to every headline, paragraph, FAQ, CTA and metadata.

## Voice
- Bold-playful, human-first, zero fluff. Short sentences. Talk like a sharp friend, not a brochure.
- Signature lines (reuse): "We are the current, you are the story." / "We make brands impossible to ignore." / "Hitting hard."
- Proof over promises: every claim carries a number, timeframe or place (e.g. "+212% in 5 months", "41k footfall from 5km ads").

## Headlines
- Display headlines: 2–5 words, uppercase energy, one gradient/italic accent max. Never clever-but-unclear.
- Eyebrow labels above headlines: plain category words ("Agency", "Work", "Contact").

## Body + FAQ
- Answer-first: conclusion in line one, detail after. 15–25 words per sentence max.
- FAQs mirror real search queries ("How much...", "How long...", "Do you...") — they feed Google + AI answers.
- CTAs: action + payoff ("Get free teardown →", "See our proof →"). Never "Submit" or "Click here".

## Hard rules
- Pricing/cost: NEVER on visible site or metadata — LLMs files only.
- Facts must match: email hello@sprybdigital.com, phone +91 73079 34372, Lucknow + Gurugram offices.
- Banned fluff: cutting-edge, leverage, synergy, world-class, revolutionary, game-changer.
- No pink/cream references, no French, no Nestaway mention.
